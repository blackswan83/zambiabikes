How to ride

On the ground

  • W pedal
  • S brake — the fix for every jump that scares you
  • A D steer, and lean into the berms
  • Space hold to squash the bike, release to hop. Let go right on a lip for extra height.

In the air

  • A D spin — 360, 720, keep going
  • W S backflip and frontflip
  • Q E whip the back end out
  • Bring it all the way round before the wheels touch, or you go down.

The bail bar

  • Every crash takes a bite. At zero, the run is over.
  • Landing tricks puts it back — the better the trick, the more you get.
  • Checkpoints top you up a little and catch you when you go down.
  • Chain tricks without crashing to stack the multiplier.

One number, one mountain

Every run in Trial is grown from a seed, so a trail code like 5PR-MAP is the whole hill: the same ridge, the same corners, the same jumps in the same places, for anyone who types it in. Beat a friend's score on their mountain, or send them yours.

The career runs five stages down the country — Nyika, the miombo woodland, the Kalahari sand belt, Batoka Gorge and the Mafinga Hills — and the seeds come from your career number, so your Batoka is nobody else's Batoka.
