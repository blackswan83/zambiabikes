    How to play

    • ▲ / W — pedal hard
    • ▼ / S — brake
    • ◀ ▶ / A D — steer down the mountain
    • Space — bunny-hop (jump!)
    • K — TURBO! Press once to open a 10-second window, then tap K as fast as you can. The faster you tap, the higher the throttle climbs and the faster you go. Then it needs 18 seconds to recharge.
    • In the air — tricks! W/S spin a front or back flip, A/D spin the bike around. Let go in time to land level — a completed flip is 150 points, a 360 spin is 120, and doing both at once is worth half as much again. Land sideways and you lose the lot.
    • B — ring your bell (if you fitted one in the Garage)
    • P or Esc — pause, restart or quit back to the menu (or tap ❚❚ on the screen)

    Follow the dirt trail — it's the fast line — or cut your own across the mountain. Hit the kickers for big air, collect copper coins, and don't hug the baobabs: they always win. Checkpoint flags save your spot if you tumble. Fastest to the finish arch wins!

    How it works

    Pick a mountain, pedal for speed and steer your own line down to the finish arch. The carved dirt trail is the fast way — grass and rocks slow you down — and the kickers are your launch ramps. Beat Arthur's ghost for silver, Armand's for gold.

    Tip from Arthur: bunny-hop (Space) just before a kicker's lip for extra air — and brake before the corner, never in it.

    Build your ride: frames, forks, tires, gears and brakes all change how the bike handles, the Tune bench sets your sag, rebound and tire pressure, and the paint booth sprays every zone down to the tire walls — wrench on it in the Garage 🔧. Dream parts are earned by riding, never bought.

          🛡️ Built kid-safe, on purpose

          Zambia Rush has no chat, no messages, no friend requests and no strangers. There's nothing to buy, no ads, and no account needed. You race ghosts — saved replays and the founders' in-game riders — never live players you don't know. Parents can read the full policy on our Parents & Safety page.
