The Garage 🔧

Build the bike, then ride it — every part here changes how your bike actually handles on the mountain. Frames, forks, rubber, gears and brakes, all with real trade-offs. Dream parts are earned by riding, never bought: that's the club promise.

🛡️ How unlocks work

A few dream parts — the downhill rigs, the champion's paint — are locked until you earn them on the mountain: finish tracks, out-ride the ghosts, hoover up copper coins. There is nothing to buy in the Garage, no coins to purchase, and no way to spend real money anywhere on this site. Wrenches up, wallets away.
