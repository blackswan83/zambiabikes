The membership form

How's your riding right now?

What happens next: your request is prepared right here, and your parent or guardian sends it to the Grown-Up Crew with one tap. A real adult reads it, then replies within 3 days to confirm — only then is membership unlocked.

The fine print, kid-sized

The Club Rules

Six rules. That's all. Armand and Arthur wrote them, the Grown-Up Crew approved them, and every member rides by them.

🪖

Helmets always

No helmet, no ride. Not even to the end of the driveway. This one is carved in stone.

👯

Buddy system

Nobody rides alone. You always have a buddy in sight, and the sweep rider has you both.

👂

Listen to guides

When a guide says stop, we stop. They know the trail, the weather and the wildlife.

💛

Kind words only

We cheer the fastest rider and the newest one exactly the same. Teasing isn't our sport.

🌳

Leave no trace

Every wrapper comes home in your pocket. We leave the trail better than we found it.

🎉

Have big fun

Whoop on the downhills. Celebrate the mud. Fun is an official club rule, not a bonus.
