How to fly

The stick

  • W S nose up and down
  • A D nose left and right
  • Q E roll — this is the one that matters
  • Arrow keys do the same as WASD.

The frames

  • Survey frames are cut long one way and narrow the other, and every one is bolted on at a different angle.
  • Roll until your wings lie along the slot, then fly through the middle of it.
  • Squared up and centred is a clean thread: more than double the points, and it patches the shield.
  • Clip the frame and it hurts. Fly round the outside and you just lose the combo.

Copper & shield

  • Fly through loose copper to scoop it. The rich seams sit out at the edge of the corridor, where the tumbling rock is.
  • Hold X to run the cutting beam: line up an orange-veined rock and it opens.
  • Space boosts and heats the drive; let it cook and it cuts out until it cools.
  • Shift brakes — the fix for every frame that scares you.
  • Every knock takes a bite out of the shield. At zero the run is over.

One number, one belt

Every run in Orbit is grown from a seed, so a flight code like 5PR-MAP is the whole sector: the same line through the rock, the same frames at the same angles, the same copper in the same seams, for anyone who types it in.

The survey runs five stages out from the lake — Kariba, the Copperbelt, the Kafue dust, the Mosi ice rings and Batoka Deep — and the seeds come from your survey number, so your Batoka is nobody else's Batoka.
