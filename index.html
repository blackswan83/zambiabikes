Zambia Rush — a full 3D ride through the Zambian wild

Arthur dreamed it up, and Armand's game rider sets the times to beat. Pedal, tuck, jump and backflip down three tracks built from the places we actually ride.

  • Five 3D mountains: miombo forest, baobab ridge, the Kasanka bat storm, the croc-lined Lower Zambezi, and Victoria Falls
  • Steer your own line, hit the kickers, dodge the baobabs
  • Race the ghosts of Armand, Arthur — and your friends, with Ghost Codes
  • Local leaderboards, first names only — no chat, no strangers, ever
  • Classic 2D version included for older devices

🛡️ Parents & guardians, read this bit

Zambia Bikes is run day-to-day by two very serious ten-year-olds — and safeguarded entirely by adults. Membership is vetted by hand, rides are guided and geared, and our online rule is simple: no chat, no direct messages, no strangers — anywhere on this site or in the game.
